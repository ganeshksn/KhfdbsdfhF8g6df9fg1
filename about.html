Our Story

Sri Visweswara Exports is a premier exporter of premium quality agricultural products from India. With over 15 years of expertise, we have established ourselves as a trusted name in the international grain trade.

Our state-of-the-art processing facilities ensure that every grain meets international quality standards. From farm to fork, we maintain rigorous quality control at every step of the supply chain.

We specialize in exporting premium rice, pulses, and flours to markets across the globe, with a strong presence in Kuwait, Qatar, and the USA. Our commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred partner for businesses seeking reliable Indian agricultural products.
